- **Mantra** /ˈmæn.trə/: 
	- A frequently repeated word or phrase that represents a core belief or guiding principle.
	- شعار - باور اصلی

- **Supervision** /ˌsuː.pɚˈvɪʒ.ən/: 
	- The act of watching a person or activity to make sure everything is done correctly, safely, or according to instructions.
	- نظارت - سرپرستی

- **Aggravation** /ˌæɡ.rəˈveɪ.ʃən/: 
	- The feeling of being annoyed, frustrated, or angry; the act of making a bad situation worse.
	- ناراحتی - کلافگی

- **Fragility** /frəˈdʒɪl.ə.t̬i/: 
	- The quality of being easily broken, damaged, or destroyed. In software, it means the code is easily broken by small changes. 
	- شکنندگی - آسیب‌پذیری

- **Inadequacy** /ɪˈnæd.ə.kwə.si/: 
	- The state of not being good enough in quality or amount; a feeling of lacking confidence or ability.
	- ناتوانی - عدم کفایت

- **Innate** /ɪˈneɪt/: 
	- Existing from birth; natural, inborn, or inherent (not learned or acquired through experience). 
	- ذاتی - فطری

- **Empirical** /ɪmˈpɪr.ɪ.kəl/: 
	- Based on observation, experience, or experiments rather than on theory or pure logic. 
	- تجربی - مبتنی بر شواهد
