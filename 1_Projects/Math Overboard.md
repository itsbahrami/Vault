### 1.1. The decimal system for whole numbers

**$10^n$**
- Read as: "ten to the n'th"
- Means: $10 \times 10 \times \cdots \times 10$ ($n$ times)
- Means: 1 followed by $n$ zeros
- Examples: 
	- $10^3 = 1,000$
	- $10^9 = 1,000,000,000$

**Expanded form**
$$ 6,725 = (6 \times 10^3) + (7 \times 10^2) + (2 \times 10^1) + 5 $$

**Number systems**

- Whole numbers: $0, 1, 2, 3, \cdots$
- Natural numbers: $1, 2, 3, \cdots$
