## A. Setup (do once, before any client)

- [ ] Intake questionnaire (the questions you must ask to fill sections 1–10)
- [ ] Blueprint template
- [ ] A simple agreement (even one page: scope, timeline, what they provide, what you deliver, revisions)

## B. Prospect contacts you

- [ ] Reply within your stated window (pick one: 24h? same day?)
- [ ] Thank them + confirm you got it
- [ ] Send the **intake questionnaire**
- [ ] Set expectations in one message:
  - what they'll get
  - how long it takes
  - what you need from them
  - what it costs (or what you want in return if free)

## C. Intake / discovery

You need this info to fill the blueprint. Get it in writing + one call.

- [ ] Business name, what they do, who they serve
- [ ] What's the site's primary JOB? (they tell you, you refine it — section 1)
- [ ] Current situation: no site? old site? social only?
- [ ] Existing assets: logo, photos, testimonials, content, domain, hosting
- [ ] Competitors they like/hate
- [ ] Budget ceiling + timeline
- [ ] Platform constraints (Iran? WebZi? WordPress? foreign?)
- [ ] Who maintains it after launch?
- [ ] Who owns domain + hosting?

## D. Draft the blueprint

- [ ] Set a deadline for yourself (Parkinson's Law — don't let it sprawl)
- [ ] Fill sections 1–10 of your template
- [ ] Section 10 (Next Steps) is the most important — that's what they actually *do*
- [ ] Sleep on it, then re-read once
- [ ] Check: is every section specific to *this* business, or generic filler? Generic = worthless.

## E. Deliver

- [ ] Send the document
- [ ] Offer a walkthrough call (30 min) — this is where the value lands
- [ ] Define revisions: 1 round included, or none
- [ ] Answer questions
- [ ] Confirm they received it and understood it

## F. Close & after

- [ ] Ask for a testimonial
- [ ] Ask permission to use as a case study (anonymized if needed)
- [ ] Ask if they know anyone else who needs this
- [ ] Ask them for feedback on *your process* — what was confusing, what was missing
- [ ] Follow up in 30 days: did they implement? what broke?
- [ ] Follow up in 90 days: what would they add now?
- [ ] Update your blueprint template with whatever you learned
